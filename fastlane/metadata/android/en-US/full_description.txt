osmodule is a third-party media client for DJI Osmo action and gimbal cameras. It browses the camera's memory, previews clips, and copies photos and videos to the phone's gallery. It needs no DJI SDK, no account, no activation and no companion service.

Features:

* Media grid with thumbnails pulled straight off the camera, grouped by capture date.
* Low-resolution streaming preview, so a clip can be scrubbed before it is downloaded.
* Optional interactive 360° playback for Osmo 360, with drag-to-look and pinch-to-zoom.
* In-preview trimming: set in and out points and download only that slice of the high-resolution clip, as a keyframe-accurate stream copy in the original quality.
* Resumable download queue that writes high-resolution files into the phone's gallery.
* Live camera status: battery, charge state, shooting mode and storage use for internal memory and SD card.
* Multi-camera support: saved cameras are remembered and the list shows which ones are currently in range.
* Mark media as favourite on the camera, and filter the grid to favourites, photos or videos.
* Module manager for optional, signed 360° viewer and Osmo 360 remote-control/GPS plugin APKs.

Supported hardware, verified on real devices: Osmo Nano, Osmo Action 5 Pro, Osmo Action 6 and Osmo Pocket 3. The same protocol is used by the Xtra-branded rebadges of those cameras (Atto, Edge, Edge Pro, Muse). Other cameras in the Osmo line are handled on a best-effort basis, and support for DJI drones using Quick Transfer is in progress.

Privacy: osmodule talks to the camera on its local, internet-less network and to nothing else. There are no analytics, no accounts, no activation servers and no cloud upload; media never leaves the phone and the camera. Cleartext HTTP is enabled because the camera serves media locally without TLS.

Requirements: Android 10 or newer, Bluetooth LE, and permission to use nearby devices (Bluetooth scan and connect) plus Wi-Fi state changes. Location permission is used for Bluetooth scanning on older releases and for the optional GPS sync feature.
